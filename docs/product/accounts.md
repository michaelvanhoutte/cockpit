# Accounts

**Managing the Account is a window over the Workspace you are in, never a screen instead of it**, and so is managing the *Cockpit*: **Platform settings** (Glossary) is on the profile menu after *Settings…*, offered to an admin only and not on a phone, and opens over the Workspace like Settings does, with **Users** and **Usage** as its sections. The header wears the Workspace's colour, holds its *Capture…* control and marks its tab, so an address reached without one had none of that: the chrome fell back to a default theme over a strip with nothing selected, which read as a second, worse header. The Workspaces and the Dashboards were such addresses, and are gone entirely — a Workspace and a Dashboard are changed on the tab each already has ("Change a workspace or a dashboard on the tab it is", issue 267). Capture is the one screen that is deliberately in no Workspace, and it heads itself rather than borrowing the band. With no Workspace at all there is nothing to draw the app around, and that is a screen of its own beside the logon page: it says so and offers the box to name your first.

**Settings is one window, opened from the profile menu**, between *Signed in as…* and *Sign out*, with the sections *Appearance*, *Connections* (Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft Teams and Text messages), *Connected apps*, *Follow-ups* and *What Cockpit remembers*. Each section is headed by its entry's name and one line on what it is for, then its areas in panels, each with a shaded header naming it (*Theme*, *Workspace*, *Address* and *Connected apps*, *How long to wait*, *Sentences*); switching section never closes the window, even while a section is still arriving. It opens on the section asked for, the only one that looks selected and the one focus starts on, and closing it puts focus back on the profile control. *Connections* belongs to one Workspace at a time: its *Workspace* panel holds the picker in its header, which starts on the open one, and the count, the connections and, while none is connected, *Add a connection* in its body: one compact row per source, its name over a sentence, each with its own *Connect*. Coming back from Microsoft or Google after connecting reopens it on *Connections* for that Workspace, saying how it went. Gmail's Connect first asks which conversations to bring in — *Labelled Cockpit*, chosen each time it opens, or *Starred (flagged in Outlook)* — then signs in with Google past its unverified-app warning, allowing Cockpit to change your mail. Outlook's Connect asks likewise, *Flagged* to start or *Category Cockpit*, with a tick box under it, *File it on the panel named after its category*, unticked to start, then signs in with Microsoft; its card says the organisation may have to approve Cockpit first. Both then ask, under their other questions, *Give it a due date*, unticked to start, and once it is ticked *days after it arrives* with a number from 1 to 365. Cancel there goes nowhere. *Text messages*, where the environment offers it, asks for a mobile number instead of leaving: *Send code*, then the code typed back and *Confirm*. A connected Gmail or Outlook row's menu offers *Change…*, the same questions starting at the answers held now and no sign-in: Save switches it, sending nothing where it is unchanged, and Cancel goes nowhere. A failing row's menu offers *Reconnect*, the same trip as Connect, starting at the row's own answer. It is a window over the Workspace and has no address of its own. **On a phone it holds *Appearance* alone**, full screen, and *Settings…* opens on it; the other sections are for a desk, by decision. At a desk it opens on the section asked for — *Connections* from the profile menu — with *Appearance* first.

**Agents (Glossary) are retired, and nothing stands in their place.** No dock, no **A**, no *Agent settings* and no Claude Code routine connection: an Item goes to Claude with **Send to Claude…** on its row's menu, which needs only Claude connected under *Connected apps*. Each Agent an account held became one remembered sentence under *What Cockpit remembers* — "Scope in Cockpit" with `/scoping {title}: {description}` became *Scope in Cockpit: Claude Code, start with /scoping* — for the Items a person captured, in every Workspace, to reword or delete like any other. An Agent deleted, with a template of the Item's words alone, a placeholder no Agent filled, too long a template, or one naming an address or a command became none. Each Workspace's routine connection was disconnected once its account's Agents were done, which forgot its credential. A **Run** (Glossary) an Agent started stays on its row, naming the Agent, until the Item's Status ends it; its session's hooks are no longer heard.

**The apps you allowed into Cockpit are seen and cut off under *Connected apps***, a section of Settings. The window says what an app you allowed may do — capture items into your Cockpit and read your items in every workspace, or only read your Panels, as the consent page said when you allowed it — then gives the MCP address to paste into claude.ai — this environment's own origin and `/mcp` — with *Copy*, at its top, shown whether or not an app is connected, and one row per app **you** allowed: its registered name, when it was connected and what it may do. An app allowed to create Items, as Claude is, shows when it last captured; one allowed only to read your Panels says *Reads your Panels* and when it last read one. *Disconnect* asks first and ends that app's access, access and refresh tokens both, at once; the Items it captured stay. **The row is gone the moment you answer *Disconnect***, whatever is read meanwhile; one the server refuses comes back, and the undo bar says why. An app somebody else allowed is not listed, even in the same Account. Not reachable on a phone, whose Settings holds *Appearance* alone.

**How long *Wait for reply…* waits is chosen under *Follow-ups***, a section of Settings: *Give it a due date [3] days after you set it*, a whole number from 1 to 365, 3 until changed. It is kept on the Account, so every device you sign in on starts the question on it, offline too, and a change reaches a tab already open on another. It is saved on Enter, on leaving the box or on closing Settings; anything else is refused beside it with *Use a whole number from 1 to 365.* and nothing saved. Under it an example row shows *Waiting since* today and the due date the number gives. **Changing it moves no due date already given**: an Item already waiting keeps its own. Not reachable on a phone.

**The sentences Cockpit keeps from your edits are seen and changed under *What Cockpit remembers***, a section of Settings. One row per sentence, newest first: its words, the kind of action it is for, whether it applies to every Workspace or one, when it was kept and how often it was used. *Edit* rewords it under the same checks Remember applies; *Delete* asks first. **Each shows at the click** — the new words are in the row, a deleted sentence is gone, and Remember closes its offer and puts the sentence first in the list — and one Cockpit refuses comes back: a rewording to its form with the reason, a delete or a Remember with the reason in the undo bar. Either asks for the open Items it fits to be proposed again, within the same ten a minute as Remember. Nothing is added here: a sentence is only kept from an edit, in the review window. The window's *Learned from you* line names the sentences a proposal was made with, as they read now, each with *change*, which opens Settings on that sentence over the window. Not reachable on a phone.
