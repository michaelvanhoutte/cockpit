How to read this

The 9 easy wins (Gmail, Telenet, all 3 Slack items, Teams, Google Tasks, Google Calendar, Billit) plus Notion action items are all doable with official APIs or open protocols. Four of them are already wired up in this very session (Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Calendar), so a first version of your dashboard could be running against those today.
The hard three (WhatsApp personal chats, LinkedIn InMail, LinkedIn connection requests) have no sanctioned way to read your personal inbox into a third-party app. Services like Unipile offer a single API that does cover them, but they work by automating your logged-in account, which violates Meta's and LinkedIn's terms and can get accounts restricted. Worth knowing the option exists; not something to build a company workflow on.
Signal and Notion mentions sit in between: technically reachable (signal-cli as a linked device; polling Notion content for @you), but you own the plumbing and there's no clean notification feed.

Suggested build order

Start with the Google + Slack + Billit cluster (one OAuth for all Google, one Slack app, one Billit key) — that's ~7 of your 15 with almost no friction. Add Telenet IMAP and Teams next. Treat WhatsApp/LinkedIn/Signal as "phase 2 / optional" and decide consciously whether the ToS risk is acceptable, or leave them as manual-check reminders in the same dashboard.

Assessment as of August 2026. API availability changes — reverify the restricted platforms before committing to a build.
