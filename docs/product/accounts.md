# Accounts

**Managing the Account is a window over the Workspace you are in, never a screen instead of it**, and so is managing the *Cockpit*: **Platform settings** (Glossary) is on the profile menu after *Settings…*, offered to an admin only and not on a phone, and opens over the Workspace like Settings does, with **Users** and **Usage** as its sections. The header wears the Workspace's colour, holds its *Capture…* control and marks its tab, so an address reached without one had none of that: the chrome fell back to a default theme over a strip with nothing selected, which read as a second, worse header. The Workspaces and the Dashboards were such addresses, and are gone entirely — a Workspace and a Dashboard are changed on the tab each already has ("Change a workspace or a dashboard on the tab it is", issue 267). Capture is the one screen that is deliberately in no Workspace, and it heads itself rather than borrowing the band. With no Workspace at all there is nothing to draw the app around, and that is a screen of its own beside the logon page: it says so and offers the box to name your first.

**Settings is one window, opened from the profile menu**, between *Signed in as…* and *Sign out*, with the sections *Connections* (Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft Teams and Text messages), *MCP*, *What Cockpit remembers* and *Appearance*. Each section is what its own window used to be and is headed by its entry's name; switching section never closes the window, even while a section is still arriving. It opens on the section asked for, the only one that looks selected and the one focus starts on, and closing it puts focus back on the profile control. *Connections* belongs to one Workspace at a time, with a picker that starts on the open one. Coming back from Microsoft or Google after connecting reopens it on *Connections* for that Workspace, saying how it went. Gmail's Connect first asks which conversations to bring in — *Labelled Cockpit*, chosen each time it opens, or *Starred (flagged in Outlook)* — then signs in with Google past its unverified-app warning, allowing Cockpit to change your mail. Outlook's Connect asks likewise, *Flagged* to start or *Category Cockpit*, with a tick box under it, *File it on the panel named after its category*, unticked to start, then signs in with Microsoft; its card says the organisation may have to approve Cockpit first. Cancel there goes nowhere. *Text messages*, where the environment offers it, asks for a mobile number instead of leaving: *Send code*, then the code typed back and *Confirm*. A connected Gmail or Outlook row's menu offers *Change…*, the same questions starting at the answers held now and no sign-in: Save switches it, sending nothing where it is unchanged, and Cancel goes nowhere. A failing row's menu offers *Reconnect*, the same trip as Connect, starting at the row's own answer. It is a window over the Workspace and has no address of its own. **On a phone it holds *Appearance* alone**, full screen, and *Settings…* opens on it; the other sections are for a desk, by decision. At a desk it opens on the section asked for — *Connections* from the profile menu — with *Appearance* last.

**Agents (Glossary) are retired, and nothing stands in their place.** No dock, no **A**, no *Agent settings* and no Claude Code routine connection: an Item goes to Claude with **Send to Claude…** on its row's menu, which needs only *MCP* set up. Each Agent an account held became one remembered sentence under *What Cockpit remembers* — "Scope in Cockpit" with `/scoping {title}: {description}` became *Scope in Cockpit: Claude Code, start with /scoping* — for the Items a person captured, in every Workspace, to reword or delete like any other. An Agent deleted, with a template of the Item's words alone, a placeholder no Agent filled, too long a template, or one naming an address or a command became none. Each Workspace's routine connection was disconnected once its account's Agents were done, which forgot its credential. A **Run** (Glossary) an Agent started stays on its row, naming the Agent, until the Item's Status ends it; its session's hooks are no longer heard.

**The apps connected to Cockpit over MCP are seen and cut off under *MCP***, a section of Settings — named for the protocol, since that is what claude.ai and every other client calls the address it asks for. The window gives the address to paste into claude.ai — this environment's own origin and `/mcp` — with *Copy*, shown whether or not an app is connected, and one row per app **you** allowed: its registered name, when it was connected and when it last captured. *Disconnect* asks first and ends that app's access, access and refresh tokens both, at once; the Items it captured stay. An app somebody else allowed is not listed, even in the same Account. Not reachable on a phone, whose Settings holds *Appearance* alone.

**The sentences Cockpit keeps from your edits are seen and changed under *What Cockpit remembers***, a section of Settings. One row per sentence, newest first: its words, the kind of action it is for, whether it applies to every Workspace or one, when it was kept and how often it was used. *Edit* rewords it under the same checks Remember applies; *Delete* asks first. Either asks for the open Items it fits to be proposed again, within the same ten a minute as Remember. Nothing is added here: a sentence is only kept from an edit, in the review window. The window's *Learned from you* line names the sentences a proposal was made with, as they read now, each with *change*, which opens Settings on that sentence over the window. Not reachable on a phone.
