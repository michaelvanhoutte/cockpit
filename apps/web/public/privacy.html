Cockpit privacy policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Cockpit is a personal inbox and dashboard run by Michael Vanhoutte. This page says what Cockpit does with your data, and in particular with your Gmail when you connect it. Questions go to vanhoutte.michael@gmail.com.

Signing in

You sign in with Google. Cockpit keeps your name, email address and Google account identifier to recognise you, and a record of each sign-in: when it happened, the country Cloudflare reports and the site that sent you. It does not keep your IP address. Sign-in records are deleted after twelve months.

Cockpit sets only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in and to finish connecting an account. It runs no advertising or tracking scripts.

What you put in Cockpit

Cockpit stores the items, workspaces, dashboards and files you create, and keeps a copy in your browser so the app opens quickly; signing out clears that copy.

Gmail, when you connect it

Connecting Gmail is optional, and you choose which mail Cockpit sees: only conversations you label Cockpit in Gmail. Google's permission for this covers your whole mailbox, because Google offers nothing narrower that can change a label, so Cockpit's limits are its own rule:

  • It reads your list of labels, to find the one named Cockpit, and the conversations carrying it. From each one it keeps the subject, the sender's name (or address where there is none), the text of the most recent labelled message, the date, and a link back to the conversation. It does not keep other messages, recipients, attachments or other labels, and it never downloads attachments.
  • It changes only the Cockpit label: it takes it off a conversation when you finish its task in Cockpit, and puts it back when you reopen it. It never sends, deletes, archives or marks mail as read.
  • It stores the sign-in Google gives it encrypted, and uses it only to do the above.

Disconnecting Gmail in Settings › Connections forgets that sign-in and revokes it at Google, and Cockpit stops reading and changing your mail. The tasks already brought in stay in Cockpit. You can also remove Cockpit's access at any time from your Google account's security settings.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare hosts Cockpit and stores its data.
  • Anthropic provides the AI model (Claude) that tidies what you capture: when an item arrives, including one brought in from Gmail, its text is sent to Anthropic's API to suggest a title, a description and where to file it. If you start an agent on an item, that item is sent to the agent you connected.
  • Google, for signing in and, if you connect it, Gmail.

Cockpit does not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Google user data

Cockpit's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features described above, is transferred to others only as described above and only to provide those features, and is never read by a person unless you ask for help with it, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Disconnect Gmail to stop Cockpit reading and changing your mail. To have your account and everything in it deleted, email vanhoutte.michael@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
