# Accounts

**Managing the Account is a window over the Workspace you are in, never a screen instead of it** — and managing the *Cockpit* is not, which is the one exception and is exactly the distinction: the admin pages (Glossary) are about who exists across every Account, so there is no Workspace behind them to keep and they head themselves the way Capture does. The header wears the Workspace's colour, holds its *Capture…* control and marks its tab, so an address reached without one had none of that: the chrome fell back to a default theme over a strip with nothing selected, which read as a second, worse header. The Workspaces, the Dashboards and the Types were such addresses; the Types are a window now, and the other two are gone entirely — a Workspace and a Dashboard are changed on the tab each already has ("Change a workspace or a dashboard on the tab it is", issue 267), and the Types keep a list because they have no tab. Capture is the one screen that is deliberately in no Workspace, and it heads itself rather than borrowing the band. With no Workspace at all there is nothing to draw the app around, and that is a screen of its own beside the logon page: it says so and offers the box to name your first.
